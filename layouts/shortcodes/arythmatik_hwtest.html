BPM counts 4 pulses per beat (16th notes). Turn the encoder on the INPUTS screen to test it; on other screens turning changes the screen.

Outputs

Outputs run only while the module shows the OUTPUTS screen.

Chase: one output after the other, LEDs 1, 2, 3 and 6 follow (LEDs 4 and 5 share the USB pins and stay dark). Follow CLK: all outputs copy the CLK input.

LED 4+5 test: LEDs 4 and 5 share their pins with the USB connection, so the module switches USB off for 10 seconds. The page waits and carries on by itself.

Loopback

Patch a cable from any output into CLK or RST. The module pulses OUT1 to OUT6 in turn and shows which output arrives. Move the cable through all six outputs, then try RST. A healthy output counts up in its cell.
